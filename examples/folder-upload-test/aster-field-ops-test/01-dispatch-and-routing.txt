Aster Field Operations - Dispatch and Routing
Record date: 2026-09-12. Scope: the Aster field operations pilot.

When a parcel is accepted, Aster Dispatch Service creates a shipment intent. It publishes each intent to Shipment Intent Topic. Aster Routing Service subscribes to Shipment Intent Topic and consumes those intents. Once it chooses a route, Aster Routing Service writes the route plan to Route Plan Database.

The dispatch service does not choose the route. The routing service uses the shipment intent as its input. This distinction matters when diagnosing a missing route plan: check the topic delivery before inspecting Route Plan Database.

This record describes the pilot workflow. It does not assign team ownership.
