Aster Field Operations - Service Ownership Register
Approved: 2026-09-15. Scope: the Aster field operations pilot.

Aster Logistics Team is responsible for Aster Dispatch Service and Aster Routing Service. Operational questions about either service go to Aster Logistics Team.

Aster Experience Team maintains Aster Tracking Service and Aster Customer Portal. The same team handles customer view incidents, while routing incidents stay with Aster Logistics Team.

Aster Returns Team is responsible for Aster Returns Service. The register does not assign ownership of Route Plan Database or either topic; those ownership details are unknown in this test set.
